# Thrive Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026 · *[简体中文](privacy-policy)*

## Short version

**Thrive collects nothing.** No account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.

## Where your data lives

Everything you create in Thrive — plant names, descriptions, photos, watering records, notes, and the device angle saved with each shot — is stored only in local storage on your own iPhone. It **never leaves your device**, and we have no way to access it.

If you have iCloud Backup turned on, your iPhone's system backup may include this data. That's Apple's backup mechanism, governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and is not something we operate.

## What the permissions are for

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Camera | Taking growth photos, and quick snapshots when you water. Photos are written straight to your device; nothing is uploaded. |
| Photo library (read) | Letting you pick an existing photo as a cover, a growth entry, or a watering snapshot. |
| Photo library (add) | Only when you tap "Save to Photos" — copies that one photo back to your library. |
| Motion & Fitness (gyroscope) | Reading your phone's pitch and roll so you can shoot from the same angle each time. The values are stored on-device alongside the photo. |

You can deny any of these; the rest of the app keeps working.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Thrive from your iPhone deletes all of it. You can also delete an individual plant or growth entry inside the app.

## Children

Thrive is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.
